Tourism Glossary

This glossary has been prepared to help travelers better understand commonly used terms in the tourism and travel industry.

Check-in

The process of registering at a hotel or before a flight.

Check-out

The process of leaving a hotel and completing departure procedures.

Transfer

Transportation service provided between airports, hotels, or other locations.

VIP Transfer

Premium transportation service provided with private vehicles.

Package Tour

A travel program that combines transportation, accommodation, and services into one package.

Reservation

An advance booking for tours, hotels, flights, or other travel services.

Early Booking

Special pricing and advantages offered for reservations made before the travel date.

Tour Guide

A professional who provides information about destinations and attractions.

Tour Program

A planned schedule of activities and visits during a trip.

All Inclusive

An accommodation system where meals, drinks, and various services are included in the price.

Boutique Hotel

A small-scale hotel offering personalized service and a unique experience.

Charter Flight

A flight specially arranged for a particular group or organization.

Travel Insurance

Insurance coverage that protects travelers against potential risks during a trip.

Visa

An official authorization required to enter a foreign country.

Passport

An official travel and identification document used for international travel.

Why This Glossary?

Understanding basic tourism terminology helps travelers make more informed decisions and enjoy smoother travel experiences.
