General Information About Türkiye

Türkiye is a unique country that connects Asia and Europe, known for its rich history, cultural heritage, natural beauty, and hospitality, making it one of the world’s top tourist destinations.

  • Capital: Ankara
  • Largest city: Istanbul
  • Currency: Turkish Lira (TRY)
  • Official language: Turkish

Transportation

Türkiye has a well-developed transportation network for both city and intercity travel.

  • Metro, bus, and tram systems
  • Taxi and private transfer services
  • Intercity buses and flights
  • Car rental services

Safety & Health

Türkiye is generally a safe country for tourists, with strong healthcare services available.

  • 24/7 medical services
  • Public and private hospitals
  • Emergency number: 112
  • Travel insurance is recommended

Money & Payments

Credit cards are widely accepted throughout Türkiye.

  • Wide credit card acceptance
  • Currency exchange offices and banks
  • Cash payment options
  • Easy ATM access

Local Culture

Turkish culture is famous for its hospitality and warm welcome to visitors.

  • Traditional Turkish cuisine
  • Cultural events and festivals
  • Handicrafts and souvenirs
  • Blend of history and modern life
